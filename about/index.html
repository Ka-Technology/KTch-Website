Our story

Small enough to care. Skilled enough to ship. We bring local businesses the technology big companies have.

Ka Technology started with four friends from Oregon State University and one observation: great young engineers were struggling to find work, and local businesses were struggling to find tech help. We set out to bring the two together.

Our first products came from real frustrations. One of us worked as a real-estate broker and watched the office’s principal broker spend close to an hour every week typing out continuing-education certificates by hand. We built RealDoc to do it in one click. A title company later used it across its Oregon branch.

Since then we’ve built AI document platforms, schedulers, gym software and websites for businesses across the Portland area. We still believe what we wrote on day one: AI is a tool, not a replacement. Its job is to give people more time, and more meaningful choices.
