name: Kept
tagline: Put money on a habit.
status: Beta
url: https://habitkept.com
year: "2026"
summary: Stake money on a habit. Finish and you get it back; miss and it goes to a charity you chose.
problem: Habits are easy to start and easy to drop. A little money on the line, for a cause you care about, changes that.
how_it_works:
  - Pick a habit (28–365 days) and a charity, and stake up to $25 on your first challenge.
  - Check in daily with a time-stamped photo or a quick AI quiz. Friends can pledge to back you.
  - Finish, and your stake comes back. Miss, and it goes to your charity. Either way, it does good.
features:
  - Pay by card or Apple Pay, or with stablecoins on Solana, where the stake waits in an escrow program that can only send it back to you or to your charity.
  - Kept never keeps the stake. There’s a small, upfront service fee.
