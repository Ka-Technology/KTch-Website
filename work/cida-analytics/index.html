CIDA Analytics

An AI platform that does the heavy parsing of HOA document dumps, so staff can focus on auditing HOAs.AI, Software · 2025–present · Beaverton, OR · CurrentBuilt with Python, React, Postgres and AI language models.

CIDA Analytics makes the CIDA Report™, which rates HOA health for Realtors, mortgage underwriters and home buyers.

Each report meant reading through an HOA’s documents by hand, from budgets and reserve studies to bylaws and meeting minutes, and CIDA wanted to grow from Oregon to the whole West Coast.

What we built

An internal platform for CIDA’s staff, now in beta testing.

  • Every document in one place

    Staff upload all of an HOA’s documents together, however many there are.

  • AI does the reading

    It sorts each document and pulls out the details the report needs.

  • People stay in charge

    Staff check each detail against its source before it’s used.

  • The report follows

    Once everything is checked, the platform scores the HOA and produces the report.

Results

  1. 1

    A modern, cloud-hosted platform built around CIDA’s workflow.

  2. 2

    Report prep moves from reading documents to checking them.

  3. 3

    Built to grow with CIDA beyond Oregon.
